Let's Talk Fashion 👗

Sometimes, you already have the colours you love. The challenge is knowing how to put them together.

Let's explore a few ways to style black, white and blue.

01. Black + White + Denim Blue

How to combine: Pair a white top with blue denim and black shoes or a black bag.

Style idea: Relaxed, casual and easy to coordinate.

02. Blue + White + Silver Grey

How to combine: Wear a blue shirt or dress with white accessories and silver-grey details.

Style idea: Light, coordinated and understated.

03. Black + Powder Blue + Cream

How to combine: Combine a black outfit with a powder-blue layer and cream accessories.

Style idea: A soft contrast between dark and light shades.

04. Navy Blue + White + Camel

How to combine: Try navy trousers with a white shirt and camel-coloured shoes or a bag.

Style idea: A classic combination for everyday or smart-casual wear.

A Little Colour Theory 🎨

Understanding a few basic principles makes choosing combinations easier.

1. Complementary Colours

These colours sit opposite each other on the traditional colour wheel. They create noticeable contrast. Blue and orange are one example.

2. Analogous Colours

These colours sit next to one another on the colour wheel. Blue, blue-green and green can create a harmonious palette.

3. Neutral Colours

Black, white, grey, beige and cream can work as supporting colours in many combinations.

4. The Three-Colour Method

Start with one main colour, add a supporting colour, and finish with a smaller accent. You don't need many colours to create a coordinated look.
